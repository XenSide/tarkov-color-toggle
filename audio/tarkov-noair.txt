# Tarkov - Stereo Bites EQ without the 10 kHz lift
#
# Same four filters as tarkov.txt with the high shelf flattened.
#
# That lift is the only boost in the whole chain, and gunshot transients carry a lot
# of energy up there, so it is the most likely single cause of shots feeling harsh.
# Removing it should calm them down - at the cost of some of the directional cue
# clarity it was added for.
#
# Compare against tarkov-softshots.txt, which keeps the lift and cuts around 3 kHz
# instead. They trade off in opposite directions, so whichever wins tells you whether
# the problem is high-frequency sizzle or midrange bite.

Filter: ON HPQ Fc 100 Hz Q 0.8
Filter: ON PK Fc 550 Hz Gain -2 dB Q 1.1
Filter: ON PK Fc 6000 Hz Gain -3 dB Q 5
