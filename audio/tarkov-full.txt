# Tarkov - full Stereo Bites chain, including the LoudMax limiter
#
# His four EQ filters unchanged, plus the LoudMax limiter.
#
# Threshold stays at his published -6.1 dB (0.797727 on LoudMax's 0..-30 dB scale):
# that is what drives the limiting and lifts quiet detail.
#
# Output does NOT. He also set it to -6.1, which caps everything 6 dB below full
# scale; stacked on top of the EQ cuts, the result was simply too quiet to play
# with. It is raised to roughly 0 dB here, leaving a hair of headroom for
# intersample peaks. This changes level, not the character of the processing.
#
# Requires LoudMax64.dll in EqualizerAPO\VSTPlugins (free, from loudmax.blogspot.com).
# Without it APO simply skips the plugin line and the EQ still applies.
#
# In his chain the limiter is there to cap what the 10 kHz lift raises, so it works
# in the opposite direction to the other presets here, which cut rather than boost.
# Judge it over a long session rather than a first impression: a limiter raises the
# average level, and that is what causes listening fatigue over time, not the peaks.

Filter: ON HPQ Fc 100 Hz Q 0.8
Filter: ON PK Fc 550 Hz Gain -2 dB Q 1.1
Filter: ON PK Fc 6000 Hz Gain -3 dB Q 5
Filter: ON HS Fc 10000 Hz Gain 3 dB
VSTPlugin: Library LoudMax64.dll Thresh 0.797727 Output 0.990000 "Fader Link" 0 "ISP Detection" 0 "Large GUI" 0
