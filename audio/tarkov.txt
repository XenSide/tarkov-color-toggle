# ("How to Hear Footsteps in Tarkov: Best EQ Settings")
#
# These four filters are his values unchanged, derived from a spectrum analysis

# so this is the honest baseline to judge everything else against.
#
# His chain also includes a LoudMax limiter, which is NOT reproduced here: he adds
# it specifically to cap what his 10 kHz lift raises. See tarkov-hd58x.txt for the
# variant with headphone compensation.
